#DESCRIPTION OF TASK 
Objective:This nail polish choosing app mockup is a sleek, modern digital experience focused on simplifying and personalizing the process of selecting the perfect nail color. The design centers around three key screens that represent the core journey for users: the Home Screen, the Product Detail Screen, and the Try-On Screen.

Try-On Screen
The Try-On Screen is the most interactive of the three. This screen uses augmented reality (AR) to let users virtually “paint” their nails using the selected polish color. When the user opens this screen, the camera view activates, and a digital overlay applies the chosen shade to their fingernails in real time.

Controls are simple and user-friendly—users can change polish colors from a quick-access palette below the camera view or switch hand orientation for better visualization. A small menu allows users to adjust lighting, save screenshots, or share the look with friends. This feature adds a fun and personal touch to the shopping experience, transforming a static product into an interactive decision-making tool.
